Project 04

Exploratory Analysis: Parts Distribution Revenue

A Python and SQL analysis of order-level transaction data to identify the key drivers behind revenue performance in a wholesale parts distribution business.

Python SQL Exploratory Data Analysis
Business Problem

A parts distribution company wanted to understand what factors drive revenue across its operations. The company sells multiple categories of automotive components through several warehouses and serves both retail and wholesale buyers.

The analysis focused on four questions:

1. Which warehouses contribute the most to total revenue?

2. How is revenue distributed across product lines?

3. How different are retail and wholesale purchasing patterns?

4. What explains large order values: higher prices or larger quantities?

Key Insights
Revenue is concentrated in two warehouses
  • Central and North warehouses generate the majority of company revenue — around $142K and $100K respectively.
  • The West warehouse contributes less than half of North's revenue, suggesting a smaller regional market or underutilised capacity.
A small number of product lines generate most revenue
  • Suspension and Traction and Frame and Body together generate a large share of total sales.
  • Demand is concentrated in two segments. Supply chain disruptions in these categories could significantly impact overall performance.
Wholesale customers drive the largest transactions
  • Wholesale orders average above $700 while retail orders average closer to $165 which is more than a 4x difference.
  • Maintaining strong wholesale relationships is critical for sustaining high revenue levels.
Higher order values are driven by pricing, not quantity
  • Higher-value products naturally produce larger order totals. Lower-priced components cluster in the $10 to $25 range while engines fall closer to $55 to $65.
  • Shifting product mix toward premium categories could increase revenue without increasing order volume.
Recommendations
  • Prioritise operational efficiency and inventory availability at Central and North warehouses.
  • Evaluate the West warehouse. Consider repositioning toward specialised inventory or regional distribution.
  • Focus sales and inventory on Suspension and Traction and Frame and Body products, the strongest demand segments.
  • Strengthen wholesale customer relationships through pricing agreements, loyalty programs, or bulk purchasing incentives.
  • Review product mix to maintain a strong offering in high-value categories like engines and structural components.
