Project 02

Data Visualisation: Global Sales Performance

An interactive Tableau dashboard analysing global revenue trends, product performance, geographic distribution and shipping efficiency from 2015 to 2017.

Tableau Data Visualisation
Business Problem

A global retail company observed a decline in revenue and profitability near the end of the reporting period. Leadership wanted to understand whether the change reflected shifts in demand, geographic concentration, product dependency, or operational factors like shipping.

The analysis focused on three questions:

1. What is driving the late-period decline in revenue?

2. Which geographies and product categories contribute most to revenue?

3. Does shipping mode affect profitability?

Key Insights
Late-period decline is driven by falling order volume
  • Revenue, profit and order quantity follow nearly identical trajectories. Margins stayed stable throughout.
  • Between September and October 2017, revenue fell by over 50% and orders dropped by over 80%.
  • This points to reduced sales activity, not pricing or cost changes.
Geographic revenue is concentrated but structurally sound
  • Top markets perform consistently at both regional and country level. Performance reflects genuine regional strength, not outliers.
  • Lower-performing markets contribute small volumes, indicating uneven geographic distribution.
  • Revenue concentration in Latin America and Europe creates exposure to regional demand shifts.
Product revenue is concentrated in one category
  • Fishing generates substantially more revenue than any other category.
  • Remaining categories contribute meaningfully but at much lower levels.
  • This creates portfolio risk if demand in the Fishing segment shifts.
Shipping performance is consistent across all modes
  • Fulfillment times and profit per shipment are nearly identical regardless of shipping method.
  • Shipping speed has little impact on profitability. The logistics process is operationally standardized.
Recommendations
  • Expand marketing in lower-revenue regions to reduce geographic concentration risk.
  • Develop mid-performing product categories to reduce dependency on Fishing.
  • Focus growth on increasing order volume through targeted campaigns and channel expansion.
  • Conduct further analysis with external market data to identify root causes of the September to October 2017 drop.
